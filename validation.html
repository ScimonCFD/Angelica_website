Validation

What each part is checked against.

Most models in Angelica carry a test that states its reference, the kind of evidence and the error obtained. This page summarises them; the full manual, with one row per check and the test to rerun it, is in the repository.

Read this first

Most checks are internal; data comparisons are few and named.

Each check carries one of these labels. A value recalled from memory is never counted as validation, and no parameter is tuned to make a check pass.

LabelMeaning
read in sourceCompared with data read in the published source (NIST, a journal table).
secondary sourceThe reference value was read where another document quotes it.
analyticalAn exact solution: Poiseuille flow, radial conduction, the method of images.
consistencyAn independent code path, a conservation law or a limit of the same model.
regressionAngelica's own stored output: guards against change, says nothing about accuracy.
not validatedNo external check; recorded with the reason.

Of 193 checks collected in the manual: 100 consistency, 33 analytical, 15 read in a source, 12 secondary sources, 6 regression, 6 not validated, 19 that cite a value without saying whether it was read, and 2 from memory.

Not validated / known deviations

What has no external check, and every recorded discrepancy.

  • Natural-gas hydrate curves, multiphase pipes (Beggs-Brill), well correlations, gas lift, compositional wells, separators, regulators and compressors: no comparison with measured data — the worked examples that would close this are not in the repository.
  • Hydrate model: 0.6–0.9 K too cold for pure methane; CO₂ above ~40 MPa about 9 K low at 100 MPa; methane hydration number 5.895 against 6.04 ± 0.03 measured.
  • Inhibitors: fitted with methane only; above 13 MPa the model's depression keeps growing while the measured one is flat (likely unsafe); MEG checked only within its own dataset.
  • Water content uses ideal vapour (underestimates at high pressure); the undersaturated hydrate check is estimated 0.6–2 K warm (safe side).
  • Laminar/turbulent friction jump at Re 2300 and black-oil viscosity below 21.1 °C: open decisions, recorded with options and numbers.
  • Computed inner film: not validated against measurements; no two-phase film or entrance effects (flagged).
The full manual — one row per check, with the reference as written, the error, the tolerance and its source, and the test to rerun — is docs/validation.md in the repository. Run one check with pytest tests/test_hydrate.py::test_methane_against_semenov_2024.
